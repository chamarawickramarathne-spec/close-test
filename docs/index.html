How a project runs.

Five steps from an idea to a closed test that stands up to a Play review.

Create the project and pay the activation fee

Sign in, add your app name and package, choose a package and pay once. The fee reserves the project and starts setup.

We prepare your Google Group

A person on our team creates a dedicated group on our Google account, confirms its settings and attaches the exact address to your project.

You connect Play Console

Add the group to your closed test in Play Console, then save your testing link in TesterLoop. The link locks once the first tester joins.

Testers join and confirm opt-in

Testers open your project in the Android app, join, and confirm opt-in. We check that their Google address is an active member of your group.

Track the clock and read reports

Once 12 testers are opted in, the 14-day clock starts. If the count drops, it resets. You see seats, days, reports and usage as they happen.

What we do not do.

Clear limits are part of the product. If one of these matters to you, ask before paying.

We do not create groups automatically

Google has no create API for consumer Google Groups, so a person creates and attaches the group. The app never claims otherwise.

We cannot guarantee Play approval

Meeting the closed-test requirement is your responsibility. TesterLoop gives you the evidence; it does not decide Google's review.

We do not monitor the whole phone

The Android app checks install status and daily foreground time only for the packages of projects a tester has joined. Nothing else is read.

Questions developers ask.

Do I need a Google Workspace account?

No. The Google Group is created on our Google account and you add its address to your Play Console closed test. You keep control of your Play listing.

What happens if a tester drops out?

Their seat is released and their own clock resets. The project still needs 12 actively opted-in testers, and the 14-day count returns to zero if the count drops below 12.

Why is there a 15-tester limit?

Every tester is paid, so a project can use at most 15 distinct testers over its lifetime. Dropped seats still count, which means a replacement must be one of the people already on the project.

How is opt-in confirmed?

The tester confirms in the Android app. Our backend then checks, through a Google Apps Script service acting as the group owner, that the tester's Google address is an active member of your group. Only an active answer confirms opt-in.

Do I need to install anything?

Yes - the TesterLoop desktop app for Windows (32-bit and 64-bit installers are published together). Testers use the TesterLoop Android app.

Can I delete a project?

Yes, until a tester's opt-in is verified. After that the project is locked for 14 days, and it cannot be deleted while a tester payout is recorded against it.
