Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

TesterLoop ("we", "us") runs a software service for Google Play closed testing, operated from Sri Lanka. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and what you can do about it. It covers the Windows apps, the Android app and this website.

Short version: we collect what is needed to run a closed test - your account, your projects, tester profiles, bug reports, and install and usage evidence for the packages a tester has joined. Card details are handled by Lemon Squeezy, not by us. We do not sell personal data.

1. Data we collect

Account data

  • Email address and password hash (authentication is handled by Supabase Auth).
  • Display name, role (developer, tester or operations) and, for developers, company name and contact email.
  • Google account email, used to match testers to Google Group membership.

Project data

  • App name, Android package name, Play testing link and project state.
  • The reserved and attached Google Group address, display name and URL name.
  • Payment status, amount, reference and, for card payments, the provider order identifier. We do not receive card numbers.
  • Payout records per tester seat, including a payment reference.

Tester data

  • Region, device list and the phone model reported by the Android app.
  • Seat and opt-in state, opt-in timestamps and verification result.
  • Bug reports you file, including severity, reproduction notes, links you paste, and the build label detected from the installed app.
  • Install status and daily foreground usage time for the packages of projects you have joined, while those projects are active.

Website and diagnostics

  • This site is static. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics scripts.
  • Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs for security and abuse prevention.

2. What we do not collect

  • We do not read whole-device usage, messages, contacts, files, location or other apps' activity.
  • We do not collect card numbers or bank details. Card data goes directly to Lemon Squeezy.
  • We do not use advertising trackers or sell personal data.

4. Legal bases

Where GDPR-style rules apply, we rely on: performance of a contract (running the service you paid for), legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, service improvement), legal obligation (tax and accounting records), and consent where we ask for it. You can withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing already carried out.

5. Who processes data for us

  • Supabase - database, authentication and server functions. Data is hosted in the region configured for our project.
  • Lemon Squeezy - card payments and tax as merchant of record.
  • Google - Google Groups and Google Play, when a tester opens a group or testing link, and the Apps Script checker used for membership verification.
  • GitHub - hosts the desktop installers and this website.

These providers may process data outside Sri Lanka. Where required, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

6. What other people can see

  • Developers see their own projects, the display name, device, seat, opt-in state and report status of testers on their projects. They do not see tester email addresses.
  • Testers see their own profile, their own joined projects, their own reports, and the public project information needed to join.
  • Operations staff see the data needed to set up groups, verify membership, record payments and payouts, and answer support requests.
  • Other users cannot see your account or project data.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account and project data: while your account exists, then deleted or anonymised on request unless we must keep it.
  • Bug reports, install history and usage summaries: for the life of the project, then deleted with it.
  • Payment and payout records: kept as long as tax and accounting rules require, even after a project is deleted.
  • Audit records: kept for security and dispute purposes.
  • Android local install history: pruned on the device after about 45 days once synced.

8. Security

Access is enforced server-side with row-level security and role checks; clients never hold a service key. Traffic uses TLS. Passwords are handled by our auth provider. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your password private and tell us if you suspect unauthorised access.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to processing. Email support@testerloop.dev and we will respond within the period required by law. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Children

The Service is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we will remove it.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be noted on this page with a new date, and where appropriate announced in the app.

12. Contact

Privacy questions: support@testerloop.dev.
