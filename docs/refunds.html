Refund Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This policy applies to the one-time project activation fee charged by TesterLoop. It forms part of our Terms of Service.

1. Before a tester joins: full refund

If no tester has joined your project, you can request a full refund within 14 days of payment. We refund to the original payment method. Setup work already done on your project (for example, a reserved Google Group) is released or retired.

2. After a tester joins: no refund

Once the first tester joins a project, the fee is non-refundable. Tester seats are paid from that fee and become committed when a tester joins, even if that tester later drops out.

3. When we refund regardless

We refund in full, even after a tester has joined, if:

  • we cannot deliver the service you paid for, for example because we cannot prepare a usable Google Group for your project;
  • you were charged twice, or charged the wrong amount;
  • a payment was not authorised by you.

4. What is not refundable

  • A project that has passed its closed test, or that you chose to stop using after testers joined.
  • A project removed for breaking our terms, Google Play policy, or applicable law.
  • Differences in bank fees, currency conversion, or taxes charged by the payment provider, which are outside our control.
  • Tester payouts already recorded against a project.

5. How to request a refund

Email primesentinel.kandy@gmail.com with your account email, the project name, the payment reference (it looks like TL-XXXXXXXX) and the reason. We reply within two business days and aim to complete an approved refund within 10 business days.

6. Who processes the refund

Card payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy as merchant of record. An approved refund is issued through Lemon Squeezy to the original payment method, so the time it appears depends on your bank or card issuer. Offline payments recorded by our operations team are refunded by the same method used to pay.

7. Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy removes rights you have under mandatory consumer law in your country. Where such law gives you a stronger right to cancel, that right applies.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The version in force when you paid applies to that payment.
